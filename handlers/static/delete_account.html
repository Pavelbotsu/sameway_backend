Delete your Sameway account

You can also do this in the app: Account & Security → Delete account.

Konto możesz też usunąć w aplikacji: Konto i bezpieczeństwo → Usuń konto.

Також це можна зробити в застосунку: Акаунт і безпека → Видалити акаунт.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email, phone, bio, photo, car details and password.
  • Your chats, location, planned trips, blocks and bug reports.
  • Pickup and drop-off points of your past rides, and comments you wrote in ratings.

What is kept

  • An anonymous ID linked to rides, star ratings and safety reports that involve other users, so their history and safety records stay intact. It no longer identifies you.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If a ride is in progress, finish or cancel it first.
