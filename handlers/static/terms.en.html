2. What Sameway is — and isn’t

Sameway is a free app that connects drivers who are already making a trip with passengers going the same way. It is available in Kraków with about 30 km around it, and in Poltava oblast, Ukraine. Sameway is not a carrier, taxi or transport company: it does not drive, own cars or employ drivers. Each ride is an arrangement between the driver and the passenger. Sameway is currently in a test phase; features may change and the service may be interrupted.

3. Who can use it

  • You must be 18 or older and give your real date of birth.
  • Give true information, keep it up to date, use one account, and keep your password to yourself.
  • You need an Android phone with the current Sameway app, internet access, location services and an email address.

4. Money: sharing costs only

Payments do not go through Sameway. A driver may agree with passengers to share the actual costs of the trip (fuel, tolls) and nothing more. Making a profit, or using Sameway for paid passenger transport (taxi, ride-hailing, commercial driving), is forbidden and may also be illegal; we suspend accounts that do it.

5. Drivers

Before publishing a route or a planned trip, a driver confirms that they:

  • have a valid driving licence for the car;
  • have valid third-party liability insurance for it (OC in Poland, ОСЦПВ in Ukraine);
  • drive a roadworthy car with a seat belt for every seat they offer;
  • only share costs, never make a profit.

Sameway does not check these documents. The driver is responsible for them and must show them to a passenger, or to us, on request. Drivers follow traffic law, drive safely, and may decline any passenger. Car details (make, model, colour, plate) must be correct, because passengers use them to recognise the car.

6. Passengers

Passengers wait at a safe place the driver can stop at, are on time, wear a seat belt, and treat the driver and the car with respect. A passenger only gets in when they feel comfortable, and may cancel.

7. How a ride works

  • A ride starts when both the driver and the passenger have accepted it.
  • At pickup, the driver tells the passenger a 4-digit code and the passenger enters it. The code confirms that you actually met; only then can the ride be completed and rated. Enter it only when you are with the driver.
  • If the passenger doesn’t turn up, the driver can report a no-show after the waiting time shown in the app; the seat is freed. A ride that is accepted but never started is cancelled automatically after 60 minutes.
  • Either side can cancel before pickup. Repeated no-shows or cancellations may lead to suspension.

8. Safety

The SOS button calls the emergency number 112. You can share your trip with someone you trust, report or block a user, and flag a ride as unsafe. In an emergency, always call 112 first. In Ukraine, follow martial-law rules, curfew and air-raid alerts: do not offer or take rides during curfew.
