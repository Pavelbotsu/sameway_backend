2. What Sameway is

Sameway connects drivers who are already making a trip with passengers going the same way. Rides are not paid through Sameway and drivers do not earn money from them.

4. Who sees your data

  • Other users: while a driver has an active route, signed-in passengers near them can see the driver's first name, photo, rating, car make/model/colour and an approximate route — its start and end are rounded to roughly 500 m and the route is shortened at both ends, so it does not show exact addresses. After a ride is accepted, both sides see each other's full name, photo, rating and live location, and the passenger sees the car's plate.
  • Service providers (processors): Google Firebase (push notifications, crash reporting, analytics with your consent, photo storage), [HOSTING PROVIDER] (servers in [COUNTRY]), [ROUTING PROVIDER] (route calculation — receives start/end coordinates), [EMAIL PROVIDER] (sign-in codes), [MAP TILE / GEOCODING PROVIDERS] (receive the map area you view and the addresses you search). 5. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time. Delete your account in the app (Account & security → Delete account) or at /account/delete. For anything else write to [PRIVACY EMAIL]. You can complain to the Polish supervisory authority, Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych (uodo.gov.pl).

6. What happens when you delete your account

We delete your profile, chats, location, planned trips, blocks, bug reports, rating comments and the pickup/drop-off points of your rides. We keep an anonymous ID linked to rides, star ratings and safety reports involving other users, so their history and safety records stay correct. It no longer identifies you.

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. [ADD: backups, access control.]
