2. What Sameway does

Sameway connects drivers who are already making a trip with passengers going the same way, in Kraków (with about 30 km around it) and in Poltava oblast, Ukraine. Rides are not paid through Sameway and drivers may only share costs, never earn money. You must be 18 or older to use Sameway.

4. Who sees your data

  • Other users. While a driver has an active route, signed-in passengers nearby see the driver’s first name, photo, rating, car make/model/colour and an approximate route: its start and end are rounded to about 500 m and the route is shortened at both ends, so it doesn’t show exact addresses. Before a ride is agreed, nobody sees your exact address, car plate or age. Once a ride is accepted, the two people in it see each other’s name, photo, rating, age (never the date of birth) and live location, and the passenger sees the car plate.
  • Service providers who process data for us:
    • [HOSTING PROVIDER] — our servers and database, in the EU;
    • Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC (Firebase) — push notifications; with your consent, statistics, crash reports and performance data;
    • Resend, Inc. — sending sign-in and password-reset emails (your email address and the message);
    • map tiles: OpenStreetMap Foundation tile servers and, for the satellite view, Esri — they receive your IP address and the map area you look at;
    • place search: Photon by komoot — receives the text you type into the search box and your IP address.
    Route calculation runs on our own servers; no outside routing service sees your trips.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it (for example a court order).

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Google and Resend may process data in the United States. Transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. If you use Sameway in Ukraine, your data is processed in the EU under this policy and the GDPR.

6. Automated decisions

Matching drivers and passengers is automatic (based on routes, distances and times). It only suggests rides; it does not produce legal effects for you, and every ride is accepted by the people themselves.

7. Your rights

You can access your data and download a copy (Account & security → Download my data), correct it, delete it (Account & security → Delete account, or on the web at /account/delete), restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] for anything else; we answer within one month.

You can complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl). Users in Ukraine can also contact the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights (ombudsman.gov.ua).

8. When you delete your account

We delete your profile, photo, date of birth, chats, location, planned trips, blocks, bug reports and rating comments, and the pickup and drop-off points of your rides. We keep an anonymous ID linked to rides, star ratings and safety reports that involve other people, so their history and safety records stay correct; it no longer identifies you. These remain only until their retention periods above end.

9. Data on your device

The app keeps your sign-in token (in Android’s encrypted storage) and your settings on your phone. Our web pages (these documents and the account-deletion page) use no cookies. Analytics identifiers are only created after you consent.

10. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. The database is not reachable from the internet, and access to production systems is limited to the operator.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the app will ask you to review and accept it again before you continue.
